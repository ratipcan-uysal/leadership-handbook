The original 94-page handbook already incorporated much of [How to be the Perfect Manager](https://ratip.notion.site/How-to-be-the-Perfect-Manager-7538cce98b604e5eb85a73e3d3b767cc), and explicitly listed it on page 88. Five overlapping original items were merged:

1. The second blocker question in One-on-Ones into "What's getting in your way?"
2. "How can I help you?" into "How can I support you better?"
3. "What is a viable alternative?" into the existing unobvious-solution question.
4. "What other options can we think of?" into the existing options/courses-of-action question.
5. The Career Development question about having the manager's position into its One-on-Ones equivalent.

Four existing prompts moved into Decision-Making; the assumption-breaking prompt moved into Creativity. Moves do not increase the item count. Three event-loop items were expanded in place. Two self-assessment items were revised to request evidence instead of inferring an employee's answer or dismissing observation.

**147 original items - 5 merged items + 36 new items = 178 revised items.** All 12 banks have use, follow-through, limits and source notes. The rest of the original question coverage remains present.

## Attribution correction

The Notion heading "A More Beautiful Question" contains a cover and material from **The Book of Beautiful Questions** (2018), a separate Warren Berger book. The [author's page](https://warrenberger.com/the-book-of-beautiful-questions/) and [publisher's page](https://www.bloomsbury.com/us/book-of-beautiful-questions-9781632869562/) confirm the title. Appendix C now explains the distinction and adds that source. The original Appendix D reference to A More Beautiful Question is retained because it is a valid, separate book, not silently renamed.

The Leading with Questions cover in the collection names Michael J. Marquardt and Bob Tiede. Practitioner attributions are retained as secondary attributions from the collection, not presented as quotations independently checked against a complete edition. The event-loop image and later prose lists lack their own explicit author credits; the revision preserves that uncertainty.
